## About
LE BRIEF is a specialized media platform and a leading press institution dedicated to delivering strategic intelligence across the energy sector, natural resources, and global economic dynamics. Operating both as a weekly analytical magazine and a real-time news outlet, LE BRIEF produces and disseminates reliable, relevant, and high-value information tailored to decision-makers.

## Mission
To deliver strategic, reliable, and real-time intelligence across the entire energy sector, empowering decision-makers, investors, and institutions to make informed decisions and gain a deeper understanding of market dynamics.

## Vision
To become a leading platform across Africa and the Middle East, seamlessly combining media and events, with the ability to connect key energy stakeholders, unlock investment opportunities, and shape the dynamics of the sector.
